Fictional stress-testing procedure. Standard example: increase every scheduled outflow by 20% over seven days.
Keep inflows and opening cash unchanged. Apply the factor to each day's aggregate outflow;
round half up to integer kopecks before rolling the balance forward.
The user can select an integer outflow increase from 0% to 100% and a horizon of 1 to 30 days.
This is a deterministic what-if scenario, not a probability estimate or a learned prediction.
Стресс: рост выплат на 20%, поступления не меняются. Срок примера: 7 дней.
