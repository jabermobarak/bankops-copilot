Fictional counterparty policy, not a Sber rule.
A single counterparty must not exceed 25% of total gross counterparty exposure in this RUB-only demo.
Calculate concentration as that counterparty's exposure divided by the total exposure, multiplied by 100.
Use unrounded values for the breach decision. A share exactly equal to the limit is allowed.
Counterparty exposure includes synthetic booked positions, not scheduled cashflow totals.
Концентрация: доля одного контрагента не должна быть выше 25% общего объёма позиций.
Если лимит превышен, нужна проверка аналитика рисков; торговые операции не выполняются.
